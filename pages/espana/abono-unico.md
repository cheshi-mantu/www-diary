# Abono Único. Spain's national transport pass

## What is it?

A subsidized 30-day unlimited travel pass issued by Spain's Ministry of Transport (Ministerio de Transportes y Movilidad Sostenible). Valid on state-operated intercity buses and Renfe trains nationwide. Extended through end of 2026.

## Pricing

| Category | Price |
|---|---|
| Adult | €60 / month |
| Under 26 (Jóvenes) | €30 / month |
| Under 14 | Free |

## What's covered

- **Renfe trains:** all Cercanías (commuter) and Media Distancia (regional) services across Spain
- **State intercity buses:** ALSA, Avanza, Interbus, Socibus, Bilman Bus and other state-licensed inter-regional operators
- Routes crossing between different autonomous communities (e.g. Valencia → Madrid, Madrid → Bilbao, Santander → Oviedo)

## What's NOT covered

- High-speed trains (AVE, Alvia, Avlo)
- Urban metro, trams, local buses (those are covered by separate regional schemes)
- Regional bus operators (e.g. PESA in the Basque Country, e.g. Bilbao ↔ San Sebastián)
- Routes within a single autonomous community operated by regional concessions

**Key rule:** if a bus route connects two different regions → covered. If it stays within one region → not covered (use Renfe instead).

## Rules & Restrictions

- **Mandatory booking:** every bus trip must be booked in advance (online or app). Just having the pass isn't enough.
- **Cancellation, buses:** cancel at least **24 hours** before departure or it counts as a no-show.
- **Cancellation, Renfe Media Distancia:** cancel at least **60 minutes** before departure.
- **3 no-shows equals ban:** three missed bookings without cancellation → pass is automatically cancelled (no refund) + 60-day ban on new passes.
- **No double-booking:** you cannot book two trips in the same direction at the same time.
- **Tickets are personal:** ID (passport, NIE, or DNI) checked at boarding — not transferable.
- **Booking fees:** ALSA charges €0 with a free Alsa Plus account. Avanza charges €1.50 per booking for seat selection.

## How to get it

1. **Under 26 only:** get a registration code first at the Ministry's portal (transportes.gob.es) to unlock the €30 rate.
2. Buy the pass on Renfe's website/app or through any participating bus operator (ALSA, Avanza, etc.).
3. Book each trip separately using your pass number + passport/ID details.
